
# Cyber Security Awareness System

## About the Project
The **Cyber Security Awareness System** is a Community Engagement Project focused on understanding common cybercrime risks and spreading practical cyber-safety awareness among the public.

The project covers common digital threats such as:
- Phishing messages and fake links
- OTP, UPI, and online banking scams
- Password theft and account hacking
- Social media scams and identity theft
- Malware and malicious websites

## Project Aim
To understand cybercrime risks, learn preventive measures related to digital safety, and spread cyber-security awareness among the community.

## Activities Covered
The project presentation describes the following activities:
1. **Police Station Visit — Shivaji Nagar Police Station:** Understanding cybercrime complaint handling, reporting online fraud, preserving digital evidence, and the role of police in investigating cyber-related offences.
2. **Court Visit — Civil and Criminal Court, Ichalkaranji:** Learning about the legal aspects of cybercrime and the importance of digital evidence in legal proceedings.
3. **Bank Visit — Central Bank of India:** Understanding digital banking safety, protecting confidential banking information, verifying suspicious messages and links, and promptly reporting unauthorized transactions.
4. **Public Awareness — Ichalkaranji Bus Stand:** Spreading awareness about phishing, OTP/UPI fraud, fake calls, and safe passwords.

## Key Cyber-Safety Practices
- Never share OTPs, PINs, CVVs, passwords, or confidential banking details.
- Verify links, calls, and messages before making online payments.
- Use strong passwords and protect personal information.
- Be cautious of suspicious links, messages, calls, and websites.
- Report suspicious or unauthorized transactions promptly to the relevant bank or official reporting channel.
- Preserve relevant digital evidence if a cybercrime occurs.

## Benefits
- Improves awareness of common cyber threats and scams.
- Promotes safer use of UPI, cards, ATMs, and online banking.
- Encourages protection of passwords and personal information.
- Increases awareness of reporting suspicious or fraudulent activity.
- Connects classroom learning with real-life cyber-safety situations.
- Helps spread cyber-safety knowledge among students and the public.
